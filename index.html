FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What courses does NoCap Academy offer? ⌄
We offer expert coaching for NEET preparation, Class 11, and Class 12 with complete subject coverage, mock tests, and personalised mentorship.
Where are your branches located? ⌄
We have two branches — India: 16-9-423/C, Old Malakpet, Near Water Tank, Hyderabad – 500036; UAE: Villa No 45, Baniyas East 7th, Abu Dhabi, UAE.
How do I book a free demo class? ⌄
Simply fill out the contact form on this page or call us directly. We'll schedule a free demo class at a time that works for you.
Do you provide study materials and notes? ⌄
Yes! Students receive handwritten notes, PDF study materials, mind maps, flashcards, and daily practice problems (DPPs) as part of the program.
Are there regular tests and performance tracking? ⌄
Absolutely. We conduct weekly tests every Sunday and provide detailed performance analysis. Parents also receive regular updates on their child's progress.
Is doubt solving available outside class hours? ⌄
Yes, we have dedicated doubt-solving sessions available daily so no student is left with unresolved questions.
